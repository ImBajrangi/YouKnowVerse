## 🏛️ Project Overview
**YouKnowVerse** is a premier, full-featured academic research support and consultancy business web platform. It establishes YouKnowVerse as a trusted, high-caliber consultancy for undergraduate students, postgraduate candidates, PhD research scholars, and university faculty.

### Core Ecosystem
1. **Public Marketing Website**: High-conversion editorial experience with services showcase, 4-step workflow, verified feedback, and research guides.
2. **Interactive Lead & Quotation Wizard**: 11-field lead capture, instant quote estimator, and direct WhatsApp routing.
3. **Scholar Workspace (Client Portal)**: Real-time project tracking across the 11 lifecycle stages, private document vault, milestone sign-offs, and project messaging.
4. **Administrative Console (Director Mode)**: Lead qualification pipeline, project progress controller, quotation & invoice manager, and live website CMS.

## 🎨 Design System & UI Principles
- **Concentric Nested Border Radius**: Strictly adheres to $R_{\text{inner}} = R_{\text{outer}} - \text{padding}$.
- **Zero Generic Hover Effects**: No artificial `translateY(-1px/-2px)` or hover scale jumps.
- **Solid Navigation**: 100% opaque headers avoiding background bleed-through.
- **Brand Palette**:
  - Primary Navy: `#071E46`
  - Secondary Navy: `#102F60`
  - Academic Gold: `#D6A52B`
  - Clean Background: `#F5F7FB`
  - Primary Text: `#17243A`
  - Accent Red: `#C83232`
